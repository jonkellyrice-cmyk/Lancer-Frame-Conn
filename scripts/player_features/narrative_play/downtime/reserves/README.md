# Narrative Play — Reserves

Future responsibility: represent mission-duration advantages earned during Downtime or granted directly by the GM.

Planned reserve families include:

- story/resources such as Access, Backing, Supplies, Disguise, Diversion, Blackmail, Reputation, Safe Harbor, Tracking, and Knowledge;
- mech equipment/gear advantages such as extra Limited uses, temporary gear, extra repairs, Core battery use, mission-specific modifications, and support equipment;
- tactical advantages such as Scouting, Vehicles, Reinforcements, environmental shielding, Accuracy, Bombardment, Ambush, alternate insertion, and NHP assistance.

The subsystem should track availability, ownership/source where useful, consumption, and mission expiration. When a Reserve maps to native Lancer Actor/Item state, prefer that native state rather than creating a disconnected counter.

Player-visible Reserve state should hand off cleanly to the GM Mission Toolkit's Preparation/Reserves views.
