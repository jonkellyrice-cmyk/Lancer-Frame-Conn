# Narrative Play — Downtime

Downtime is a **sub-feature family of Narrative Play** covering the time between missions, whether that interval represents a few hours or several months.

Downtime should support both freeform roleplay and structured mechanical actions. The structured system exists to create persistent story/campaign state and Reserves; it should not replace ordinary roleplay.

Planned subfolders:

- `downtime_actions/` — reusable structured downtime action definitions and their roll/outcome workflows.
- `reserves/` — mission-duration advantages earned or granted through downtime and other fiction.

Downtime should integrate forward into the DM Mission Toolkit's Preparation and Reserves stages while remaining a player-facing Narrative Play experience when pilots choose and resolve their own downtime actions.
