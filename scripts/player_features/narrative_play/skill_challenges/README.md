# Narrative Play — Skill Challenges

Future responsibility: support group and extended narrative challenges where success depends on a series of skill checks rather than one roll.

Planned support includes:

- define the challenge goal and stakes;
- identify participating characters;
- record individual successes/failures;
- determine group challenge outcomes from the proportion of successful rolls;
- support ties or special adjudication where the rules call for them;
- support extended challenges with multiple stages/rounds of rolls;
- track progress such as completed stages, wins/losses, current complications, and remaining objectives;
- keep the state visible to players while preserving GM-only information where necessary.

The challenge layer should compose ordinary Narrative Play skill checks rather than duplicate their roll logic.
