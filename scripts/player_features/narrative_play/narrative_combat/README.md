# Narrative Play — Narrative Combat

Future responsibility: support fights and dangerous action scenes resolved through Narrative Play rather than tactical mech combat.

Planned support includes:

- player initiative rather than mech-style initiative turns;
- player-declared goals and GM-established Risky/Difficult/Heroic framing;
- NPC behavior determined from player outcomes rather than independent tactical turns;
- narrative Harm as a consequence when appropriate;
- pilot weapons/gear influencing fictional positioning and consequences without recreating tactical weapon combat;
- transition into tactical mech combat when mechs are involved or when the table wants structured tactical resolution.

This feature should remain clearly separate from the Hardsuit tactical-pilot combat mode even though both use pilot Actors.
